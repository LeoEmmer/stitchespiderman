## The build in one paragraph

Grammar: **Split Stage**. The whole page is one before/after seam: the divider is the
chrome (Before/After tags ride it), each act moves the split, and the story is the car
crossing the line. Six pinned acts (spans 1.5 / 1.7 / 2.0 / 2.8 / 1.9 / 1.25 = 11.15vh):
Recognition (4-plane parallax hero) → Tension (kinetic words, the dull side grows) →
The Wash (the page's only scrub: the pilot's 71-frame sequence in the after column) →
**peak: the Inspection Light** → Range (price ledger revealed on the winning side) →
Commitment (the split collapses to the finished car). Tail: reviews + the pilot's
booking form, same behavior. Signature move: at the peak, the seam becomes the
detailer's inspection light — a bright core + halo that sweeps the panel; left of the
beam the same frame sits under a dull "swirled" grade, right of it in full gloss, and
on fine pointers the visitor can drag the light. Mobile recomposes: the split turns
horizontal (before on top), the light falls from the top, headlines and ledger restack.

Zero paid generation: every photo is a frame of the pilot's free Pixabay clip
(VariousPhotography, Pixabay Content License), reused as-is. Fonts are self-hosted
(Sora + Inter variable files as data URIs in `fonts.css`, ~99 KB): zero third-party
requests, and the type renders identically in the packed mirror.

## Verification (skill harness, scripts/shoot.mjs)

Chromium: Playwright cache build (the Muse browser binary refuses third-party
launch: "Hatch WebGL domain policy is required"). Passes run: canonical desktop
1440×900, canonical mobile 390×844, canonical reduced-motion, mirror desktop,
mirror mobile — 37 samples each.

- **No dead scroll** in any pass; every sample settled.
- **Contrast:** every cue clears 4.5:1 at its worst frame, all passes.
- **Console errors:** none after self-hosting the fonts (the first pass flagged the
  Google Fonts request, which this VM's proxy refuses inside Chromium; that failure
  is what moved the fonts in-house).
- **Feel check (cold read of the sheets, one word per act):** Recognition, Unease,
  Relief, Awe, Confidence, Resolve — matches the curve in `BRIEF.md`; the peak is the
  longest act and the only interactive one.
- **Live check:** the public URL returns 200 with the right title, and all 15
  published files are byte-identical (SHA-256) to the locally verified mirror. The
  harness browser itself cannot traverse this VM's egress proxy to re-shoot the
  public URL (ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED), so byte identity + the local mirror
  passes are the evidence chain.
- Fixes found by the strips and applied before shipping: kinetic `lines` destroys
  authored line breaks (switched to kinetic `words` with tuned wrap widths); the
  range ledger's left-wipe reveal saws words at rest (switched to `up` reveals with
  tighter windows); hero copy overlapped the photo panels (headlines moved to the
  top mat, panels lowered); tension panels now use a dry frame (f004) against a
  beaded-gloss frame (f060) so the two states actually contrast.
