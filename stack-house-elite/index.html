The build

Five layers. Zero shortcuts.

Every Stack is built the same way, in the same order, every time. This is the whole recipe — the order matters.

01

The foundation

Brioche, Toasted in Butter

Baked in-house every morning and toasted cut-side down in butter, so the base stays crisp under the juice.

Baked fresh daily

02

The main event

The 500° Smash

Fresh chuck, smashed thin on a ripping-hot flat top. Maximum contact, maximum crust, ninety seconds a side.

Never frozen

03

The melt

Double Cheddar, Under the Dome

Two slices of American cheddar, a splash of water and the dome — sixty seconds of steam until it pours over the edges.

Double always

04

The crunch

Fresh Stuff, Cut at Sunrise

Shredded lettuce, tomato and pickles prepped every morning. Cold and crisp against the hot patty — that's the contrast.

Prepped at sunrise
