The Listing

One house. One ridge. No neighbors in the frame.

Built in 2019 and held by one family since, The Ridge House pairs a sculptural modern form with the quietest street on the Tiburon ridge.

Price$4,850,000
Bedrooms5 bd
Bathrooms6 ba
Interior6,200 sqft
Lot0.8 acre
Built2019

From the street, it reads as a quiet white silhouette. Inside, the west wall disappears — glass from floor to ceiling, the Bay laid out below like a second floor plan. The great room, kitchen and primary suite all face the sunset; the bedrooms face the sunrise and the ridge.

Meridian Estates is presenting The Ridge House by private appointment only. Tours are scheduled at golden hour whenever possible — that is when the house makes its own case.

Details

The house, room by room.

Five stops on the tour, in the order your advisor will walk them with you.

01

The arrival

Walls of glass

Floor-to-ceiling west glass across the main level. The Bay is not a view from this house — it is the artwork the house was framed around.

02

The gathering

The great room

Double-height ceilings, a sculptural fireplace, and seating that never has to turn away from the water. Seats twenty, feels like four.

03

The ritual

Chef's kitchen

Italian cabinetry, a twelve-foot island, and a hidden catering kitchen behind it — built for dinners that end on the terrace.

04

The retreat

Primary suite

A private wing with its own sunset terrace, spa bath in honed stone, and a dressing room lit like a gallery.

05

The evening

Pool & spa terrace

A heated pool and spa set into the ridge, lit at dusk, screened from every sightline. The last stop of the tour — and the one nobody wants to leave.
